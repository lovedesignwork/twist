For an evening trip from Patong to Phuket Old Town, **check a live journey estimate, add a buffer before your reservation, and decide how you will return before leaving**. A prearranged car or a suitable ride-hailing option can simplify the trip, but availability, fares and pickup conditions vary. Do not build a sunset booking around a fixed travel time found in an old itinerary.

Patong and Old Town are separate parts of Phuket. Treat the journey as part of the outing rather than a quick move between neighbouring streets. A little planning lets the restaurant and the walk remain enjoyable even if the road takes longer than expected.

## Choose the main reason for the trip

Start by deciding what you are travelling for: sunset, the Sunday market, dinner or a general look around Old Town. That decision determines how much of the evening needs a fixed time. A sunset has a deadline; a flexible street walk does not.

If TWIST is the main destination, choose your arrival window and [request a table](/reserve) before arranging the final departure time. If you want to explore first, give the walk its own space rather than using it as an activity squeezed between the drive and dinner.

A group should agree on the same priority. If half the guests want a long market visit and the other half expect an early dinner, the problem cannot be solved by driving faster. Choose a shared plan or arrange separate meeting times clearly.

## Use the real pickup and destination

Enter your actual pickup point in the transport app or booking request. “Patong” is too broad to produce a useful estimate. Likewise, distinguish the Old Town street you want to explore from the restaurant you will eventually visit.

TWIST's published address is 154 Phang Nga Road, Phuket Old Town, with the venue on the 19th floor. Use the [official contact page](/contact) and its directions when confirming the destination. Save the address before the journey so you can refer to it if mobile data becomes unreliable.

Check the pickup pin, not just the written address. A pin on the wrong side of a busy road, inside a restricted area or on an inaccessible lane can create confusion. Choose a legal stopping point and communicate through the transport provider's supported contact method when needed.

## Separate travel time from arrival time

A navigation estimate usually describes the road journey. Your evening also includes waiting for the car, meeting the driver, getting out, finding the entrance and reaching the venue. Those steps are why arriving at the building at the reservation time can still feel rushed.

Check the route again near departure. Traffic, rain and local events can change the picture, and an estimate is not a guarantee. Add a margin appropriate to how important your arrival time is. For sunset, use our [rooftop timing guide](/blog/phuket-rooftop-sunset-time) to work backwards from the light.

If the journey starts running late, contact TWIST with a realistic update. Ask what can be accommodated instead of assuming that any table will remain available indefinitely. Clear communication is more useful than repeatedly revising an optimistic arrival estimate.

## Pick a transport arrangement that suits the group

A prearranged car can be useful when several people want to travel together or when a defined return plan matters. Confirm the passenger count, pickup location, waiting arrangements and what is included in the quoted service. Do not assume luggage space or an extra stop is included.

A ride-hailing option may suit a more flexible evening, subject to availability where and when you need it. Review the vehicle and booking details within the app, and follow the provider's pickup instructions. If the group needs multiple cars, assign the meeting point before everyone leaves separately.

Public transport may fit some journeys, but only if a current official timetable and route match both your outward and return plans. Do not rely on a daytime service continuing into the late evening. Check rather than improvise after dinner.

## Adjust the plan for Sunday

The Sunday Walking Street can affect the final approach to parts of Old Town. A pedestrianized street is not a suitable car pickup point simply because the map pin allows you to select it. Ask the driver where a legal and accessible meeting point makes sense.

Leave time to walk out of the crowded area before the pickup. Keep the group together or share a clear meeting point so that one person is not holding the car while the others browse. If someone has difficulty walking, consider this before choosing a market-first plan.

Our [Sunday market and dinner guide](/blog/phuket-sunday-market-dinner) helps you choose the order of the evening. It is often easier to make one stop the priority than to give the market and a sunset reservation equally tight deadlines.

## Make the return journey part of the plan

Before dinner, decide whether you are returning together and whether the return is already booked. If using a prearranged driver, confirm how changes or waiting are handled. If booking a ride later, leave room for availability to differ from the outward trip.

Keep your phone charged and save the address of your destination in Patong. A low battery at the end of the night can make a straightforward pickup unnecessarily difficult. Carry only what you can comfortably manage while walking and entering the car.

If anyone plans to drink, the return should not depend on that person driving. Choose a transport arrangement that lets everyone follow their own pace at dinner. The view is more enjoyable when getting home has already been considered.

## Keep the evening worth the journey

You do not need to cover all of Old Town to justify coming across the island. A short walk and a good dinner can be a complete outing. Our [Old Town after-dark guide](/blog/phuket-old-town-at-night) offers a compact approach that leaves time to sit down.

Look at the [TWIST menu](/menu) before departure, especially if you are travelling with mixed appetites or dietary requirements. Knowing that the meal suits your group is more useful than adding another stop just because it appears nearby on a map.

## Quick questions

### How long does Patong to Old Town take?

There is no reliable single duration for every pickup point and evening. Check a live route estimate close to departure and add time for waiting, access and reaching the venue.

### Should we book the return in advance?

It depends on your flexibility and group needs. If timing or travelling together is important, discuss a return arrangement beforehand. Otherwise, keep a realistic margin for finding a suitable ride.

### What should we send with a reservation request?

Give the party size and intended arrival time, then explain any important seating or dietary needs. [Contact TWIST](/contact) if your travel plans change so the team can advise on the booking.
